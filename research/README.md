# Research & ideas

Where we park ideas, customer evidence, and decisions so the whole team can discuss and track them.

- `landscape/`: desk research on existing products and competitors. Latest: [similar solutions (2026-10-01)](landscape/2026-10-01-similar-solutions.md).
- `evidence/`: real customer conversations, observations, and cited sources (add as we collect them). Only real evidence goes here, never invented quotes or stats.
