# Mission 7: Product Demo & Pitch (due Oct 6, 200)
URL: https://acthouse.notion.site/Mission-7-Product-Demo-Pitch-92d4a99790e082c5849d01c2f6e66385

Mission Objective: Demonstrate the Product and Tell the Evidence-Backed Story
Complete the pitch deck begun in Mission 6 with the working product at the center. Demonstrate the coded digital MVP performing a real customer task with AI functioning inside it. Explain what customers actually did, how the team changed the product, how it could reach and sustain use, and what remains uncertain. The deck and video are required; neither replaces the usable product or evidence. There are no no-code or non-digital MVP exceptions.

Quote: "The strongest pitch is a working product, a real customer response, and a team that knows what to build next."

Deliverable: Product Demo & Pitch Package
- A working product link or safe access route, a reviewable editable-code/project route, and a truthful fallback if the live demonstration fails. Never share secrets or credentials.
- A repeatable demonstration of the core task: starting point, customer action, meaningful result, the in-product AI function and customer control, and an error/help state.
- A completed 8–16-slide pitch deck connecting problem, customer evidence, working product, observed use, adoption or sustainability logic, team, and next step.
- A 3–4-minute pitch/demo video showing the current release, with a usable viewing link in the packet.
- A truthful build-and-use record: at least 10 target customers contacted about the MVP, responses and actual product attempts counted separately, what happened in those attempts, the resulting revision, and what is live, simulated, untested, or broken.

Action Items (summary): prepare the working demonstration from a new customer's entry point on the intended device, full task incl. AI + error/help state, no password or private data needed by the viewer, one meaningful path not a tour, name human-supported/simulated edges when they appear, honest backup. Complete, check, rehearse: 8-16 slides + 3-4 min video on the current product; seven-mission arc (team and problem; customer discovery; value and adoption; coded AI-enabled MVP; observed use and revision; next experiment or ask); outreach vs replies vs invitations vs attempts; direct customer words only with permission; AI as skeptical rehearsal partner via FACTS; rehearse speaker/operator handoff, fallback, answers on evidence, business model, AI behavior, privacy, accessibility, next steps.

Example numbers (illustrative, not program results): 12 contacted, 5 responses, 3 permitted attempts.
