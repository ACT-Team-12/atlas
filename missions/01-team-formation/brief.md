# Mission 1: Team Formation & Build Readiness

**Due:** September 30, 2026 by 11:00 p.m. ET · **Points:** 125 (added after staff review)
**Submit:** ATL Cup 2026 submission page. One packet file with team name and mission title.

## Objective

Understand how teammates think and work, then turn those strengths into a team that can build, test, and release one coded digital product with AI performing a meaningful function inside it. ACT roles (Architect, Creative, Techie) are contribution lenses, not fixed labels.

> "A team becomes real when everyone can help move the product forward."

## Deliverable: Team Build-Readiness Plan

- Team name, members, ACT contribution areas, strengths, interests, and 3–5 important gaps or support needs.
- Owners and backups for product decisions, editable code, customer contact, testing and release, and team communication.
- A working agreement covering decisions, check-ins, disagreement, rest, and how the team unblocks someone who is stuck.
- Confirmation that the build owner and backup can open the editable code workspace. Report access status, never credentials.
- One provisional customer task to investigate in Mission 2.

## Action items

1. **Build the team plan.** Each teammate identifies skills, experiences, interests, one skill they want to practice, and any access need they choose to share. Map contributions across Architect, Creative, and Techie. Assign owners and backups for the five responsibilities. Finish together: "We think [TARGET CUSTOMER] needs to [TASK] when [SITUATION]." Treat it as a hypothesis.

## Pro tips

- Use ACT roles to expand participation. Everyone can contribute to coding, design, research, testing, and the pitch.
- Give every critical responsibility a backup.
- Ask for help as soon as a tool, device, access, or team blocker appears.
- Keep the first customer task narrow enough to investigate in Mission 2.
- Mission 5 outcome is a working coded digital MVP with AI inside. No no-code or non-digital exception.

## Example

- **Provisional customer task:** "We think a local shop owner needs to turn a customer question into a clear next action while serving customers."
- **Shared ownership:** Techie owner maintains the build; Creative owner shapes the customer-facing experience; Architect owner coordinates evidence and scope. Each has a named backup.
- **First build check:** Build owner and backup open the same starter project and run it.
