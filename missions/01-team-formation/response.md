## Working agreement

- **Decisions:** Discuss important decisions as a team and aim for consensus. Vote if consensus isn't reached.
- **Check-ins:** Brief check-ins on what we're working on, what's done, and whether we're blocked. Slack messages or Zoom calls.
- **Disagreements:** Focus on the idea, not the person. Everyone explains their reasoning; evidence, customer feedback, and competition goals guide the decision, with a vote for the final call.
- **Rest:** Respect reasonable breaks and personal time. Say when you need to step away instead of feeling pressured to be always available.
- **Getting unstuck:** If someone is stuck for more than a reasonable time, they tell the team. Another member (the task backup or whoever has time) helps brainstorm, clarify, or break the task into smaller steps.
- **Communication:** Communicate changes, deadlines, and concerns early, and assume positive intent.
- **Accountability:** Everyone owns their tasks and flags early if they need help or can't meet a deadline.
