# Mission 3: Customer Research & User Personas

**Due:** October 2, 2026 by 11:59 p.m. ET · **Points:** 150 (added after staff review) · **+100 if Airtable receives it 2 hours early**

## Objective (verbatim)

Turn Customer Evidence into a Product Change. Create a detailed ideal customer persona (ICP) to understand better who your customer is and their pain points.

Learn what two relevant types of potential customers are trying to do, where they get stuck, and what your product should do differently.

Complete the customer profiles yourselves from real, permitted evidence before asking AI to critique them. Then change the coded product path and, where possible, observe a target customer trying it. The outcome is a better product decision, not a polished fictional persona.

## Deliverable: Customer Evidence & Product Change (verbatim)

Submit one packet file containing:
- Two concise Ideal Customer Persona
- One priority customer need and the decision it creates for your product's core task. Identify how AI can help you solve for this.
- Develop 4 to 5 customer personas using AI from your original customer persona. Test the Imagination.

## Action items (summary of the Notion steps)

1. **Identify your two primary users.** Two different types, for varied perspectives.
2. **Build each persona:** demographics; motivations and goals ("what success looks like"); behaviors and habits (how they solve it today, where they spend time, decision style); values and influences; barriers and frustrations.
3. **Extract pain points:** recurring problems across personas, most painful or urgent, gaps current solutions don't solve. Prioritize 3 to 5.
4. **Package it:** Persona 1 (name, story, details), Persona 2, Pain Points List (3 to 5). Visuals, mock profiles, "day-in-the-life" welcome.
   - Pro tip: explain why their challenges matter to the venture.
- **Refine personas / behavioral insights from real users:** unexpected behaviors, how we validate persona and journey assumptions.
- **Touchpoints and moments of truth:** critical interactions, moments with the biggest effect on satisfaction.
- **Pain points and opportunities:** where the journey frustrates most, how the product helps.
- **Go deeper, know the market:**
  - market gaps (existing solutions that fall short and why; underserved segments);
  - Blue Ocean (uncontested space; eliminate, reduce, raise, create);
  - unique value proposition;
  - scalability and barriers to scaling.

## Pro tips (verbatim)

- The team's own reasoning comes first; AI assesses and expands it.
- A generated persona is a hypothesis, not customer evidence.
- Keep each profile short enough to guide a build decision today.
- Remove identifying or sensitive details before using external AI tools or sharing the packet.
- Finish with a visible product change and an honest account of customer reaction, not only two profiles.
