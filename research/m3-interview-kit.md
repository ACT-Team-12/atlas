# Mission 3 interview kit (customer research)

Goal: talk to real people and build personas only from what they say. Judges score "evidence of user value" first.

## Who to talk to (aim for 5+ total, mix of these)
- **Community health workers / patient navigators** (primary user). Places in our area from the verified list: Mercy Care (Chamblee and other sites), Ethne Health (Clarkston, many refugee patients), MedCura Health (Doraville, Northlake), The Family Health Center at West End, Healing Community Center. Call the main number and ask for the CHW, navigator, or social work team.
- **Nonprofit staff** who help people get care or benefits: food bank benefit navigators, refugee resettlement case workers, church health ministries.
- **Patients or caregivers** who have had a hard time following up after a visit (friends, family, classmates count if they really did it).
- **Anyone clinical we already know** (Anusmita's workplace, family members who are nurses), as long as they speak as themselves and agree to be quoted or kept anonymous.

## Before you start (say this)
"Hi, I'm a student on a team in the Atlanta Innovation Cup. We're learning how people follow through after a clinic visit, like getting labs done or getting to a referral. Can I ask you 5 to 10 minutes of questions? I won't share your name unless you say it's okay. Please don't tell me any patient's personal health details."

Write down: date, role (not name unless they agree), how you reached them, and whether we can quote them.

## Questions (ask about real past events, not opinions about our idea)
1. Tell me about the last time you helped someone (or you yourself) after a clinic visit. What did they need to do afterward?
2. Which step most often doesn't happen: the lab, the referral, the pharmacy, or the follow-up visit? What stopped it the last time?
3. What gets in the way most: rides, cost, insurance, language, work hours, phone or internet, not understanding the paper, or not knowing what help exists?
4. What do you use today to find help (211, websites, a spreadsheet, calling around, memory)? What's annoying about it?
5. How long does it take you to put a plan together for one person?
6. What would you need to trust a tool that reads the visit paper? What would make you never use it?
7. (Only at the end) Show the live site with the sample paper. What's confusing? What would you change first?

## After each conversation
- Add a file in `research/evidence/YYYY-MM-DD-role.md` with what they actually said (quotes only if they agreed).
- Note one product change it suggests. We'll ship the top ones and say so in the packet ("we changed X because a CHW told us Y").

## Don't
- Don't invent quotes, people or numbers. If we only get 3 conversations, we report 3.
- Don't collect real patient records or health details.
